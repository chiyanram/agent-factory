5 · Bugs the audit found

  • Workers can't post status. post-status and forge.py aren't in the worker image, yet G9e makes post-status the only way a worker reports.
  • Tokens leak into every process in the box. The entrypoint exports CLAUDE_CODE_OAUTH_TOKEN, GITLAB_TOKEN and GH_TOKEN as env vars.
  • Budget pause is off by default unless FLEET_WINDOW_TOKENS is typed. G14 has no off state.
  • The watchdog runs only when the model remembers to call it. No timer schedules fleet-watch.
  • Host-wide managed settings from the old fleet may also apply to the new coordinator. Unverified.
