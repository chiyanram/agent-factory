## Where ours is behind the best source

1. **Docs drift against code.** Ours checks links, words and MUSTs, but no check ties a doc to the code it describes. AO has drift gates on its contract layer, and Sandcastle has a test that ties pages, TOC and glossary together. The count drift above shows the result.
2. **Per-run learning signal.** Devin's Session Insights flag misleading knowledge and rewrite the prompt for each session. Our `retro` starts only by hand, and only after a failure repeats on 2 merged tickets.
3. **Outcome metrics.** Optio computes a success rate and Factory.ai links spend to PRs and issues. Our merge rate and fix minutes are not computed (`measurement.md:14`), and our run records do not name the model.
4. **Generated, refreshed knowledge.** Factory.ai's AutoWiki refreshes on every push, and Devin has DeepWiki. Our brief is written once, and the review does not read it.
5. Ours leads on evals: no other source has a reviewer golden set, a baseline or an eval gate on a change.
