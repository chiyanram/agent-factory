## Where ours is behind the best source

1. **Human PR comments never reach the implementer** (`build-path.md:52`). Devin, AO, Optio and Symphony all route
   review comments back to the run; ours routes only its own Act on findings. The fenced, typed `task.md` path is the
   safe channel to extend (`/act` is already decided).
2. **Pre-PR state lives on one machine** (#498). Optio (Postgres + CAS + `task_events` with the user) and AO (SQLite
   outbox with retries) keep one durable store; ours loses the ticket if that machine is gone.
3. **No worker → coordinator progress channel.** AO's `ao report` outbox and Devin's wake-on-child-finish tell the
   coordinator what happened; ours learns only from `board` / the ping, and the coordinator has no typed event feed.
4. **No auto-split of large work.** Devin managed Devins, Factory.ai Missions/Workstreams and Sandcastle's
   parallel-planner decompose and fan out; ours splits into child tickets by hand (AGENTS.md MUST, #557).
5. **Cleanup step is manual** (`build-path.md:26`), where the rest of the chain is code-driven.
