## Operator rules — follow them exactly

- **Always through wayfinder / grilling.** One question at a time, recommendation first, "yes" accepts. Never decide
  on your own; discuss, compare with the references, then the operator decides. Don't deviate from the map.
- **Learn the WHY, not the rule.** References change on measured evidence; keep their reasons. A piece enters the
  design only if it traces to an issue we hit or a practice ≥2 independent sources share (CP4).
- **Quality bar:** pstack and mattpocock — small markdown, clear instructions, built from the references.
- **Scope:** generic but not too generic — start from the operator's needs (agent-platform + small work), usable by
  another developer on their own laptop; office work later.
- **Every research gets its own ticket** and records every detail in a numbered file under research/.
- **Hosting is in scope** even though no agent named it — agents can't see their own hosting problems.
- **Pull everything into context;** don't narrow to one issue. Use the old map's research and learnings (#11 index).
- **Commits and pushes only on the operator's explicit "yes" in this session.** No AI attribution trailers in
  agent-platform; agent-factory commits so far carry the Co-Authored-By line.
- **Reporting style:** diagram/table first, ~3 lines of prose, one thing per reply; every ticket/MR number with 2–6
  words of context; never a bare "A or B?" — give the state, the options and a recommendation.
- Never use chiyanram-kit.
